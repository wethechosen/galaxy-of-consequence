# Galaxy of Consequence

Main timeline: 150 ABY, following the Yuuzhan Vong invasion and Darth Krayt's era.
Legends/Legacy is the chronological spine; compatible Canon and EU enrich it.
Contradictory histories cannot all be simultaneously true. Every activated lore
claim needs source review. Our 150 ABY developments are campaign continuations.

The galaxy is uneven. There is no required main quest, allegiance, starting
planet, companion, Force destiny or Sith reveal. The player reviews a starting
situation based on their own biography. XIII and IGFED are campaign drafts:
thirteen hidden Sith masters exploiting reconstruction, not the cause of every
problem. A player may never encounter or discover them.

## Accepted choices

- Solo, local React app and SQLite persistence; narrative travel and combat.
- Saga Core rules with reviewed compatible Saga supplements. Other systems:
  lore and flavor only. Point buy after source verification.
- Mature tone, lethal Saga consequences, rules-valid baseline resources.
- Independent NPC companions; orders do not guarantee obedience.
- Optional action suggestions; free-text intentions always supported.
- Journal automatically records discovered leads; tracking never accepts a job.
- Death: successor in the same world, checkpoint branch, or separate campaign.
- Offline time: 7 campaign days per real day; full catch-up on return.
- Personal danger and irreversible loss pause for a decision. Dependent events
  also pause; unrelated events may continue.
- Economic rates come from recorded contracts, not invented Saga rules.
- OpenAI adjudicates through server validation; Nemotron supplies constrained
  NPC flavor. Live AI is disabled by user choice; test providers only for now.

## Gates

Extraction is not verification. No campaign turns or stat creation until the
rules engine and source review are complete. A rulesStatus database field alone
must never enable provisional keyword-based adjudication.
