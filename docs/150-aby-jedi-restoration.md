# Galaxy of Consequence — New Jedi Order (150 ABY)
**GM authority dossier · Star Wars Legends history and GOC-continuation strictly separated**

## Definitive published background (130–139 ABY)

* **130 ABY — the Massacre at Ossus.** The One Sith attacked the Jedi, killed Kol Skywalker and began the Third Jedi Purge. The Jedi retained survivors but were not a fully recognized galactic institution under Krayt.
* **137 ABY — Hidden Temple Council.** K'Kruhk, Tili Qua and T'ra Saa were Council Masters at Taivas, maintaining Jedi continuity despite exile. These are period-specific memberships, not proof of a 150 ABY appointment.
* **138 ABY — assault on Taivas.** The Hidden Temple was evacuated and T'ra Saa died protecting the survivors. Taivas must not be presented as a thriving 150 ABY headquarters.
* **138 ABY — end of purge at Coruscant.** Cade Skywalker killed Darth Krayt; Wolf Sazen died fighting Darth Stryfe. The former Sith Temple on Coruscant was retaken and returned to Jedi control.
* **138 ABY — Galactic Federation Triumvirate.** The Jedi, Fel Empire and Galactic Alliance formed a shared government. Jedi Master K'Kruhk represented the Jedi alongside Empress Marasiah Fel and Admiral Gar Stazi. The Jedi Order regained public institutional standing and was not vassalized by the Fel Empire.
* **~139 ABY — Darth Wredd's insurgency ends on Mala.** Wredd used the scattered One Sith as his target; a major Sith force was destroyed. Neither an exact Sith casualty census nor a guarantee every Sith is dead exists in the Legends endpoint.
* **139 ABY — Imperial Knights reform announced.** Marasiah Fel announced plans to remove absolute personal loyalty to the sovereign after Mala. This is historically **an announcement**; a 150 ABY completion/implementation timetable would be a GOC-specific development, not a published fact.

## The actual GOC Order in 150 ABY

The **New Jedi Order is an independent major faction**, not a tiny destroyed remnant and not a subordinate unit of either the Fel Empire or Imperial Knights. The Jedi are a founding component of the Triumvirate yet retain their own Force ethics, diplomatic standing, educational traditions, personnel, culture, relations and institutional plans.

**Institutional seats are deliberately distinct from private NPC profiles:** a high council exists as a tradition, but GOC has NOT yet recorded whom it appointed in 150 ABY, whether K'Kruhk still occupies the Triumvirate seat, who holds the rank of Grand Master, exact Jedi population, Temple payroll, fleet or financial budgets. They may be established by a dedicated GOC authoritative historical transaction, never invented by a dialogue or roster read.

### Seven independent offices, no automatic appointments

1. **High Council:** deliberates ethics, education, conflict resolution, major policy; no guaranteed veto over allied governments.
2. **Jedi Triumvirate delegation:** represents the Jedi's independent constitutional and humanitarian role. K'Kruhk's **138 ABY** service is established, not automatically eternal.
3. **Temple stewardship and archives:** oversight of reclaimed Coruscant facilities, book recovery, protected artifacts and evidentiary access.
4. **Education and welfare:** novice admission, guardianship, age-appropriate training, qualified mentors and ongoing ethical development; no forced recruitment or automatically learned Force powers.
5. **Healing and civilian restoration:** medical and ecological aid with permission of local partners; no inexhaustible cures or forced partnerships.
6. **Archives and field research:** scholars and investigators who verify authenticity before alleging Sith criminality.
7. **Mediation and civilian protection:** lawful Jedi field missions distinct from imperial policing or military command.

### Core GOC Jedi

The registry's complete mechanically validated original profiles remain authoritative:
- **Akkan Vuu** — Aqualish novice/apprentice; studies restraint and supervised training.
- **Tarvyn Dree** — Mirialan relic investigator; requires evidence and proper custody, cannot arbitrarily designate someone Sith.
- **Kala Morrin** — Bothan senior scholar/archivist; not yet appointed current head of Ossus.
- **Shado Liang** — Ithorian healer/ecological restoration adviser; no fictitious automatic high rank.

This is the **named GOC cast**, not a census of the Jedi Order. The wider institution includes multiple independent scholars, service personnel, novices, mentors and local missions; populations should be modeled in aggregate until individual identities matter.

### Historical Legends people in a GOC simulation

- **K'Kruhk** — founder-era 138 ABY Triumvirate representative. **150 ABY standing not confirmed**, so no Grand Master, term extension or death may be invented.
- **Tili Qua** — former High Council Master in the Hidden Temple; later role unknown.
- **T'ra Saa, Wolf Sazen, Kol Skywalker** — **deceased**, never spawn as living in 150 ABY.
- **Cade Skywalker** — independent Force-user in latest relevant material; do not assign him to Council leadership or conscript him into the Order.
- **Shado Vao** — historic Jedi figure; no invented 150 ABY rank or survival certification.

## Long-term faction behavior

The Order may independently plan:
- Temple, scholarly, and archive recovery with staff and realistic material expenses.
- Voluntary apprentice intake, mentorship and wellbeing without forced destiny.
- Civilian medical and agricultural relief.
- Evidence-based investigation of artifact provenance.
- Deliberation about the Triumvirate, Fel Knights and civic jurisdiction.
- Jedi ethical self-review, with credible disagreements but without automatic schism.

Planning happens on GOC's **authoritative clock**, using `lib/faction-strategy-150aby.ts`. Objectives are proposals only; they **do not** silently issue missions, discover the XIII, prosecute D'mir, grant XP, change ranks, seize property, or modify a campaign ledger. Material outcomes need source-backed independent NPC actions, resources, validation, Saga mechanics and authoritative commit. No plot armor, predetermined Sith nemesis or forced story intersections.
