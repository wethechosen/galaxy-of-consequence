## I. Public-facing dossier

Lady Seralyth Vhaelor, sixteen, was born into a family whose connections span Hapan court, internal security and military command. Her mother, Lady Tazielle Vhaelor, stands within the political circle of the expected successor, but neither mother nor daughter has been declared heir. Her father, Lord-Commander Rethan Vhaelor, has built a career around household and Consortium security.

Tall, nearly D'mir's height, Seralyth has exceptionally dark-brown, flawless skin, dark amber-brown eyes and a magnificent, long, very full curly afro reminiscent of Diana Ross's classic silhouette. She wears fine high-court fabrics and fitted high boots whose soles will not betray her on polished stone. Her beauty can invite underestimation, but carries **no free mind control, attack bonus or species statistic**. Elegant manners are a cultivated skill. Her cruelty is selective, controlled and precise, never random sadism. She loves fine clothes, court formality and the ritual of being perfectly prepared; none makes her less dangerous.

A typical appearance: plum silk coat with a disciplined waist, long draped panels with subtle gold geometric stitching, narrow dark trousers and polished black boots. At public court she wears no Sith sigil and displays no lightsaber.

## II. Protected program — only the AI GM knows

A tiny Hapan inner circle identified Seralyth's Force sensitivity and approved an extraordinarily private state-security training path. Senior patrons imagine they are developing a capable Hapan counterweight to off-world Force dominance and the biases of larger dynasties. They do **not** understand that an outside Sith Lord has infiltrated the deeper instruction.

The Sith Lord teaches by intermediaries, withholding his identity, operating method and purpose. Seralyth is suspicious, but she is still dutiful and sees the curriculum as the price of protecting her family. Her training includes covert travel, interrogation resistance, defensive lightsaber forms, court intelligence, refined deception, practical restraint and assassination. Her *red* single-bladed lightsaber is kept hidden. She must conceal the weapon, her affiliation and her proficiency.

Her Soresu technique is remarkable by the standards of an adolescent student, even frightening to experienced combatants. This does **not** mechanically award her the Jedi Knight-only Soresu talent; her live sheet has lawful Block and Deflect talent defenses, plus concealment tools. This distinction protects the rules and preserves her growth.

### The same Sith Lord, and what really happened to Kelvek

The outside instructor is the **same Sith Lord who led the dark-side prison gang** involved in targeting Kelvek during D'mir's confinement.

Kelvek had worked at the InterGalactic Banking Clan and **really did skim credits** from the orders he serviced before displacement. Many displaced Muuns later found work with IGFED. Kelvek instead became a **paid staff librarian** at Coruscant's Juvenile Detention Center—never an inmate. At least one person with post-IGBC knowledge suspected the surviving hidden fortune. That intelligence reached the Sith Lord, who made the prison gang believe Kelvek was worth targeting. The exact informant, means of communication, sums and disposition of the money are **unknown**. Do not rewrite the fact of the fortune as a fabrication, or assume D'mir inherited or can access it.

Seralyth has **no knowledge whatsoever** of Kelvek, D'mir, the gang's earlier operation or its relationship to her master. D'mir has no knowledge of Seralyth. The master is **not automatically Darth Korvalis**; his identity must be established through continuity evidence rather than name-matching.

## IV. GM scene: "The Unmarked Envelope" — Hapes

**Status: prepared, not played.** This is a scene seed in Seralyth's separate Hapan thread. It neither places her in D'mir's current Bonadan scene nor resolves her choice.

Morning comes through the high windows of House Vhaelor in sheets of pale gold. Outside, the terraces run down toward fountains and the bright water of Hapes; farther away, traffic rises from court landing platforms in quiet ribbons. 

Seralyth stands at an old mirror, fastening the cuffs of a plum-colored court coat. Its tailored hem skims the tops of her high black boots. Long dark curls rest free around her shoulders. Her reflection shows a noble daughter on the morning of a formal audience. There is no weapon in sight.

A sealed court invitation rests on the dressing table. Beneath it lies a second envelope, plain, unaddressed and delivered through a security entrance normally used only by her father's officers. It was placed exactly where she would notice it.

Her mother's knock is soft. Lady Tazielle crosses the threshold and notices both pieces of paper. For a moment she says nothing. Then: "There are people at court who mistake discretion for obedience. Remember the difference."

As the doors close, a solitary training chime sounds deep in the estate. It stops before the second note.

**GM next decisions, not preselected:** investigate the anonymous envelope and verify its provenance; accompany Tazielle into public court and learn what power is moving; attend the private Soresu session and face a proper tactical test; interview the security officer who accepted the delivery. Her reactions follow her personality and events, not an author-mandated ending. The scene is only committed to her own event ledger **when the GM actually runs it**.

## V. Long-term threads — GM-only possibilities

Seralyth and D'mir are peers in age and may encounter each other by a valid route. Seralyth can introduce him to **Cloak** practice and sophisticated concealment as earned instruction; she cannot simply grant mastered Force powers or retroactively make D'mir greater than Palpatine. Their deepest trust and any later romance must develop in play, beginning with distance and competing loyalties.

A future investigation may reveal their shared enemy. If D'mir uncovers sufficient evidence, their relationship may permit an attempt to persuade Seralyth to turn against her master. A confrontation and his death **require scene actions, earned discoveries, combat rolls and persisted outcomes**. If events go differently, the AI GM must accept that. Do not script a triumph.
