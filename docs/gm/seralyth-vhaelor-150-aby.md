## 1. Appearance and public role
Lady Seralyth Vhaelor is a tall, sixteen-year-old dark-brown-skinned Hapan Human, nearly D'mir's height, with flawless luminous complexion, dark amber-brown eyes and a long, full natural curly afro in the silhouette of Diana Ross. Her elegance is unmistakably Hapan: exquisitely fitted jewel-tone court clothing, refined gold detailing and tall, practical boots. A practiced smile and stillness disguise a dangerous defensive fighter. The attire is designed for movement, not exhibition armor. The red single-bladed lightsaber exists only in secure private training; she NEVER wears it visibly at court.

Mother: Lady Tazielle Vhaelor, a noblewoman with privileged access to the circle around the presumptive next successor, but **not an automatically established heir**. Father: Lord-Commander Rethan Vhaelor, senior Hapan military/security officer. House Vhaelor is a GOC-original house, not a claim to published Legends royalty. She is publicly a brilliant court-security student.

## 3. Sealed background: GM eyes only
Hapan rulers and internal-security networks have reason to distrust outside Force dynasties and the exceptional privileges associated with the Skywalker/Jedi tradition. A secret, contested security faction sees Seralyth as a native Force-sensitive weapon that Hapes might one day control. The Queen Mother has not openly established such a program; most of court knows nothing.

A foreign Sith has insinuated himself into the girl's instruction. Hapan officials do not know his genuine identity or purpose. She is loyal to the discipline but increasingly suspicious of the instructor's secrecy and conflicting demands. Her mastery of Soresu is disciplined defense, not a license for indiscriminate slaughter.

**The concealed Kelvek connection is real:** her Sith master is the same Lord who directed the dark-side juvenile prison gang and wanted access to Kelvek's fortune. Kelvek truly skimmed the financial orders he serviced before IGBC Muun displacement; most Muuns moved into IGFED employment, but Kelvek became a **staff librarian**, not a detainee, at the Coruscant juvenile detention center. The Sith had credible intelligence or inference that Kelvek held billions. The **origin of that intelligence** remains a live mystery, not a fabricated rumor. Seralyth has never been told that connection. D'mir knows nothing of her.

## 4. Opening episode, Hapes — “The Hall Without Windows”
*Third-person GM-controlled scene, before either character knows the other. This is not D'mir's current scene.*

One bell sounded in the pale gallery of House Vhaelor. Attendants carried away the last of the court invitations, leaving the reflecting pool to catch the gold of the screened lamps. Seralyth remained motionless in a court gown cut to accommodate riding boots. Her long curls brushed her shoulders as she looked toward three silent training emitters.

The first machine fired a padded dart. Seralyth moved only enough to let it pass. The second fired twice, faster. She neither flinched nor reached for the weapon she was forbidden to show. When the third emitter began its sequence, she felt the old temptation to answer with anger. Instead she let it empty itself against the practice screen.

“Again,” said a voice from beyond the privacy wall. “This time give the observer nothing at all.”

She breathed until the heat inside her dulled. A visitor would see a young noblewoman rehearsing poise. No weapon. No teacher. Nothing to survive a question.

An aide entered with Lady Tazielle's sealed summons: the west salon, before nightfall. His eyes lingered on the equipment, then withdrew.

“I'll attend,” Seralyth replied.

After his footsteps faded, she turned toward the wall. “If your orders conflict with my mother's, whom am I meant to obey?”

The silence gave no answer.
