# Galaxy of Consequence | Alien NPC story dossiers

## 150 ABY campaign (Kelvek's flashback takes place in 147 ABY)

These stories adapt the original user-supplied GOC supplements to the confirmed 150 ABY campaign. The source documents' potential 200 ABY titles, powers and careers are not automatically transferred to 150 ABY.

**Rules status:** Levels, abilities, skills, talents and Force powers are proposed GM character ratings, NOT fully committed mechanical builds. The actual save and validated advancement engine control all outcomes. No Human racial bonus applies. Mirialan and Kiffar web rules are fan-created and are not enabled as mechanics.

## The 17 individual story campaigns

### Rena Salvek — Imperial Knight trainee (proposed level 4 Jedi)

**Allegiance:** Fel Imperial Knights. **Species:** Zeltron. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

**Origin:** Rena was recruited from Zeltros because she could sense distress long before her teachers explained the Force. Her empathy helped her protect the weak but made the rigid etiquette of Bastion's training halls especially difficult. A senior Knight taught her to distinguish compassionate judgment from surrendering discipline.

**First chapter:** An Imperial Knight training crystal disappears from a secured rack. Rena volunteers to preserve the scene and investigate without accusing the cadets who envy her sensitivity.

**Second chapter:** The investigation tests whether she can remain loyal to Imperial procedure while fairly hearing a vulnerable trainee. Her private correspondence with a Jedi friend creates an apparent but unproven motive.

**Long-term campaign:** Establish the crystal's fate, show an accountable chain of custody, protect the innocent, and earn a substantive Knight assignment through play; she is not yet a commissioned senior Knight.

**Flaw:** Seeks the approval of authority and may interpret another's fear as guilt.

**Ability ratings:** STR 12, DEX 14, CON 12, INT 11, WIS 12, CHA 16.
**Skill focus:** Use the Force, Perception, Persuasion, Initiative. **Talent direction:** Force Perception and disciplined defensive training.
**Force powers (candidate only):** Move Object, Negate Energy.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** charisma +2, wisdom -2. **Biology:** Empathy; Soothing pheromones (not Force powers).

### Loresh Kallun — Veteran frigate captain (proposed level 9 Soldier)

**Allegiance:** Fel Imperial Navy. **Species:** Duros. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** none.

**Origin:** Loresh started as a navigation officer under fire and learned that a captain's duty is to preserve the people aboard his vessel, not to decorate a service record. During the Sith-Imperial conflict he diverted an evacuation route to save refugees, a decision still buried in an old inquiry.

**First chapter:** He must shepherd a civilian convoy past unstable patrol coordinates with a frigate too small to intimidate every threat.

**Second chapter:** Corporate escorts want speed, Imperial superiors demand obedience, and a crew member questions his old evacuation record. He must verify the danger rather than charge blindly.

**Long-term campaign:** Bring the convoy through, account for losses and decisions, and prove he deserves a larger naval command. The future Imperial Virtue Star Destroyer remains unearned.

**Flaw:** Excessive caution sometimes lets a nimble opponent control the tempo.

**Ability ratings:** STR 10, DEX 16, CON 11, INT 16, WIS 14, CHA 12.
**Skill focus:** Pilot, Knowledge (tactics), Use Computer, Perception. **Talent direction:** Battle Analysis and disciplined fleet command.
**Force powers (candidate only):** None.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** dexterity +2, intelligence +2, constitution -2. **Biology:** Expert Pilot.

### Ardis Nefar — Espo investigations lieutenant (proposed level 7 Soldier)

**Allegiance:** Corporate Sector Authority / Espo. **Species:** Zabrak. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** none.

**Origin:** Ardis grew up among Iridonian traders who believed corporate order was better than chaos. He joined the Espo forces for stability and learned how quickly corporate procedure becomes an instrument of intimidation.

**First chapter:** Cargo manifests implicate a falsified industrial shipment pattern. His superiors want a profitable answer immediately; Ardis must find real admissible evidence.

**Second chapter:** A promised off-ledger detention shortcut threatens the case, forcing him to choose between unquestioning promotion and credible due process.

**Long-term campaign:** Expose or disprove the freight fraud and submit a case that survives review. A captaincy and broad command authority are future possibilities.

**Flaw:** Treats witnesses as subordinate employees and confuses coercion with certainty.

**Ability ratings:** STR 15, DEX 13, CON 14, INT 12, WIS 12, CHA 10.
**Skill focus:** Perception, Knowledge (tactics), Persuasion, Use Computer. **Talent direction:** Commando and disciplined security tactics.
**Force powers (candidate only):** None.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** None. **Biology:** Heightened Awareness; Superior Defenses (+1 racial to Reflex, Fortitude and Will).

### Akkan Vuu — Young novice Jedi repair apprentice (proposed level 3 Jedi)

**Allegiance:** New Jedi Order. **Species:** Aqualish. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

**Origin:** Akkan was raised among marine workers on Ando, where neglected equipment could cost a family its livelihood. A Jedi relief team recognized his sensitivity after he stayed to repair a flooded life-support system rather than flee.

**First chapter:** At Ossus he wants to learn repairs under supervision. His curiosity outruns his training, especially around devices with restricted access.

**Second chapter:** A damaged public water-filtration mechanism forces him to request permission, follow safety procedure, and work alongside people who distrust an Aqualish initiate.

**Long-term campaign:** Complete his supervised training and demonstrate safe mastery of a real repair without claiming access to Sith vaults or Jedi Knight powers.

**Flaw:** Curiosity leads him to touch a mechanism before seeking authorization.

**Ability ratings:** STR 14, DEX 12, CON 16, INT 12, WIS 12, CHA 9.
**Skill focus:** Use the Force, Perception, Knowledge (life sciences), Mechanics. **Talent direction:** Awareness and control before combat prowess.
**Force powers (candidate only):** Move Object.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** constitution +2, wisdom -2, charisma -2. **Biology:** Amphibious biology; Species Toughness (verify before granting extra feat).

### Tarvyn Dree — Independent Jedi relic investigator (proposed level 8 Jedi)

**Allegiance:** New Jedi Order (strained). **Species:** Mirialan. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

**Origin:** Tarvyn survived years of rumors about Sith infiltration and now sees patterns of corruption almost everywhere. He was trained to investigate relics, not to issue private death sentences, yet the fear of another purge has become his defining weakness.

**First chapter:** A Sith-era artifact arrives at the Ossus public records portico with an incomplete provenance trail.

**Second chapter:** He must interview collectors and test documents without coercion. One witness has a useful lead and a perfectly ordinary reason to fear a Jedi investigator.

**Long-term campaign:** Secure a verifiable lawful chain of custody and protected archive transfer; he cannot assume the rank of Jedi Shadow or execute suspected Sith without real adjudication.

**Flaw:** Interprets uncertainty as a concealed hostile plot.

**Ability ratings:** STR 11, DEX 16, CON 12, INT 14, WIS 15, CHA 11.
**Skill focus:** Use the Force, Perception, Stealth, Knowledge (galactic lore). **Talent direction:** Force Perception and measured inquiry.
**Force powers (candidate only):** Move Object, Negate Energy, Force Stun.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** dexterity +2. **Biology:** Mirialan cultural tattoos; no homebrew racial mechanics enabled.

### Vesla Kreel — Early Sith acolyte (proposed level 4 Jedi)

**Allegiance:** Murkhana-associated covert sect. **Species:** Twi'lek. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

**Origin:** Vesla escaped work gangs on Ryloth only to become indebted to collectors who claimed they could secure freedom for her sibling. Her early Force instruction made her dangerous enough to be useful, not powerful enough to be free.

**First chapter:** Sent to a Bonadan broker to trace a relic courier, she realizes the sect is treating her sibling as leverage.

**Second chapter:** She has to decide what evidence would actually free the captive without surrendering more secrets to handlers.

**Long-term campaign:** Resolve her sibling's captivity and negotiate or fight her way out of coercive obligations through playable choices. A Sith Apprentice prestige class and mentoring relationship with Korvalis are not yet established.

**Flaw:** Her terror of abandonment can override caution; combat training is narrow.

**Ability ratings:** STR 10, DEX 14, CON 12, INT 12, WIS 12, CHA 16.
**Skill focus:** Use the Force, Deception, Stealth, Perception. **Talent direction:** Force Sense and restraint under pressure.
**Force powers (candidate only):** Force Grip.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** charisma +2, wisdom -2. **Biology:** Twi'lek species traits (individual effects require engine implementation).

### Alar Mar'Isk — Kiffar interpreter and cult agent (proposed level 5 Noble)

**Allegiance:** Independent Sith cult recruiter. **Species:** Chiss-raised Kiffar. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** latent.

**Origin:** Raised among the disciplined traders of Chiss frontier space, Alar adopted their cautious use of information but never possessed their ancestry. A secretive Sith broker rewarded his skill at recruiting skilled civilians.

**First chapter:** He needs a slicer who can translate a mundane public map index without learning the purpose of the cult's hidden shipping route.

**Second chapter:** The slicer wants a verifiable contract, while Alar holds a relic fragment he has not reported to his handlers.

**Long-term campaign:** Build his independent network and decide who owns the fragment without claiming Korvalis's trust or automatic Force psychometry.

**Flaw:** His rhetorical skill makes him overestimate his ability to control better informed patrons.

**Ability ratings:** STR 10, DEX 12, CON 12, INT 14, WIS 11, CHA 16.
**Skill focus:** Persuasion, Deception, Knowledge (galactic lore), Use Computer. **Talent direction:** Negotiation and influence rather than unearned Sith talents.
**Force powers (candidate only):** None.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** None. **Biology:** Kiffar is biological ancestry; Chiss is upbringing, not a hybrid racial bonus; No psychometry without a verified power.

### Sia'thra Velle — Senior trade adviser (proposed level 8 Noble)

**Allegiance:** Hapan diplomatic delegation. **Species:** Falleen. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** none.

**Origin:** Sia'thra left Falleen court traditions to study the mismatch between Hapan sovereignty and the Corporate Sector's appetite for favorable contracts. A Hapan minister hired her because she could see the cost hidden inside ceremonial language.

**First chapter:** She must negotiate an initial review of commercial passage rights on Bonadan without signing away future concessions.

**Second chapter:** A sealed annex might expose a conflict of interest, but she cannot treat access to a letter as ownership of its contents.

**Long-term campaign:** Produce an authorized preliminary agreement that protects Hapan trade autonomy. Baroness and ambassador are not automatically her 150 ABY titles.

**Flaw:** Disdains visible emotion and underestimates people who refuse to be flattered.

**Ability ratings:** STR 10, DEX 12, CON 12, INT 16, WIS 10, CHA 18.
**Skill focus:** Persuasion, Deception, Knowledge (bureaucracy), Knowledge (social sciences). **Talent direction:** Lineage, educated diplomacy, hard negotiation.
**Force powers (candidate only):** None.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** charisma +2, wisdom -2. **Biology:** Falleen pheromones are physiological (requires Fortitude/condition rules, not Force); Hold Breath.

### Ko'ra Veil — Owner-operator freighter captain (proposed level 5 Scout)

**Allegiance:** New Jedi Order. **Species:** Bothan. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

### Shado Liang — Jedi healer and agricultural adviser (proposed level 8 Jedi)

**Allegiance:** New Jedi Order. **Species:** Ithorian. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

**Origin:** Shado was raised on Felucia by Ithorian seed conservators and came to the Jedi after an epidemic ruined food crops. Their first loyalty is to the patients and farmers whose lives depend on lasting, practical relief.

**First chapter:** A Dantooine relief council needs to select a viable crop without wasting the settlements' limited irrigation supplies.

**Second chapter:** Local growers distrust outsiders and have good reasons to challenge Shado's laboratory assumptions.

**Long-term campaign:** Earn consent, demonstrate a sustainable crop trial, and show improved access to food. Shado is not yet Keeper of the later enclave and cannot use unimplemented healing or plant-manipulation powers.

**Ability ratings:** STR 10, DEX 11, CON 14, INT 14, WIS 18, CHA 15.

### Darth Xeran — Dark-side relic scholar (proposed level 9 Jedi)

**Allegiance:** House Murkhana, privately aligned. **Species:** Chiss. **Era:** 150 ABY. **Force:** trained.

**Origin:** A Chiss scientist trained to distrust prophecy, Xeran lost colleagues while chasing unverified ruins and gradually accepted Sith patrons who offered forbidden instruments. His studies are detailed, but the leaps in his conclusions often exceed the evidence.

**First chapter:** He has a fractured mineral sample and a location claim that may point toward an ancient relic.

**Second chapter:** A rival researcher offers confirmation at a price; Xeran must distinguish real material evidence from planted Sith symbolism.

**Long-term campaign:** Authenticate the artifact and choose its custody through encounters. His apprentice relationships, Rakatan powers and relic bonuses are not automatically his at campaign start.

**Flaw:** Obsessive pursuit of a discovery makes him vulnerable to deliberately fabricated proof.

**Ability ratings:** STR 10, DEX 15, CON 12, INT 18, WIS 15, CHA 13.
**Skill focus:** Use the Force, Knowledge (galactic lore), Use Computer, Perception. **Talent direction:** Force Sense, Telekinetic Power and analytical research.
**Force powers (candidate only):** Force Grip, Move Object, Force Lightning.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** intelligence +2. **Biology:** Bonus Trained Skill; Low-Light Vision; Sith title implies no automatic invulnerability.

### Kelvek — Imprisoned former finance executive and mentor (proposed level 8 Noble)

**Allegiance:** Former IGBC, detained independent archivist. **Species:** Muun. **Era:** 147 ABY. **Force:** none.

**Origin:** On Muunilinst, Kelvek once helped manage structured credit arrangements for influential firms until he found that debt instruments were being used to capture public offices. A quiet refusal to erase an audit trail ended his career. He was imprisoned, then assigned to maintain the detention complex's library.

**First chapter:** In the prison library in 147 ABY, he protects access to lawful study materials and teaches an unusually attentive young detainee how ownership and stewardship differ.

**Second chapter:** Outside agents try to exploit Kelvek's former contacts. Every lesson he shares must avoid exposing people whose lives depend on confidential records.

**Long-term campaign:** Preserve the library and pass on disciplined financial thinking before his established later death; this is a past-tense flashback, not a living 150 ABY participant. D'mir's memories of Kelvek remain the authority for their personal history.

**Flaw:** His pride in intellectual control makes him slow to acknowledge what others learn from pain rather than books.

**Ability ratings:** STR 9, DEX 10, CON 11, INT 19, WIS 15, CHA 14.
**Skill focus:** Knowledge (bureaucracy), Knowledge (social sciences), Use Computer, Persuasion. **Talent direction:** Educated and commercial negotiation.
**Force powers (candidate only):** None.
**Species bonuses proposed for later rules validation:** strength -2, intelligence +4. **Biology:** Impose Order; Iron Will (+2 Will); No Sith Force powers.

- The 34 real NPC save campaigns remain separate from D'mir's primary continuity.
